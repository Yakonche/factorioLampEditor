## Factorio Lamp Editor 1.3.0

This release makes text and emoji stamps predictable across operating systems
while retaining native platform rendering when it is legally available.

Based on the original [Factorio Lamp Editor](https://github.com/jojkos/factorioLampEditor)
and its [web application](https://factorio-lamp-editor.vercel.app/).

### Highlights

- Font choices are now separated into bundled, detected system, and imported
  sources, with monospaced and proportional subgroups.
- Iceberg, Jersey 10, MedievalSharp, Quantico, and Space Grotesk are bundled
  with their original SIL Open Font License 1.1 notices.
- Noto Sans JP remains bundled and the default text size increases from 14 to
  18 pixels.
- Emoji rendering can use the automatic OS-native style, Apple Color Emoji on
  Apple systems, Segoe UI Emoji on Windows, or bundled Noto Color Emoji on
  every platform.
- The selected emoji style applies to the catalog, curated presets, static
  stamps, and every frame of animated emoji.
- Apple and Microsoft font files are never redistributed; unavailable native
  styles are clearly disabled and the open Noto renderer remains available.

Cette version sépare les polices intégrées, système et importées, ajoute cinq
familles sous licence OFL, passe la taille de texte par défaut à 18 et propose

Emoji est intégré au portable ; les fichiers propriétaires Apple et Microsoft
ne sont jamais redistribués. Le style choisi s’applique aussi aux animations.
