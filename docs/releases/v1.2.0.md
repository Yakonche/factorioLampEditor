## Factorio Lamp Editor 1.2.0

This release improves the editor and generated blueprints for exceptionally
large animations such as Bad Apple, without reducing their resolution or FPS.

Based on the original [Factorio Lamp Editor](https://github.com/jojkos/factorioLampEditor)
and its [web application](https://factorio-lamp-editor.vercel.app/).

### Highlights

- Large controller previews now sample ROM footprints across the complete
  animation instead of clustering the first 5,000 deciders at one end.
- Bad Apple combinators and deciders are therefore visible across their real
  controller area in the editor while preserving responsive rendering.
- Controller substations now use a spanning-tree topology instead of a fully
  cyclic grid, removing redundant copper and circuit-wire connections.
- The optimized topology preserves electrical coverage, clock distribution,
  circuit reach, and all four controller orientations.
- Windows release packaging now verifies that the Electron runtime is present
  before invoking electron-builder.

### Bad Apple validation

The development fixture was validated at its original 360×270 resolution and
20 FPS: 3,118 sampled frames, 3,095 unique frames, and 650,104 generated
deciders. The optimized top controller removes about 19,050 redundant support
connections without changing the animation data.
