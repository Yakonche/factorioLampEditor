Factorio Lamp Editor offers the official Twemoji 17.0.3 static PNG artwork as
an optional emoji style. Assets are downloaded from jsDelivr on demand and
stored in the user's persistent emoji cache. The artwork is not stored in this
repository or bundled inside the executable.

No endorsement by Twitter, X, jdecked, or the Twemoji contributors is implied.
Downloaded artwork is resized and converted into Factorio lamp pixels when a
user creates a text stamp with the Twemoji style.
