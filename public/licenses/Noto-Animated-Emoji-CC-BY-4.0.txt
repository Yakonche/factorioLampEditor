Factorio Lamp Editor displays the official catalog and downloads the selected
animation asset from fonts.gstatic.com on demand. Used assets are retained in
the user's persistent emoji cache for offline reuse. The animation artwork is
not stored in this repository or bundled inside the executable.

No endorsement by Google is implied. The downloaded artwork is resized and
converted into Factorio lamp frames when the user creates an animated stamp.
